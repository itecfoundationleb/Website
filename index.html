Our beginning

Founded to connect learning, entrepreneurship and real professional growth.

ITEC Foundation Leb was founded on 20 May 2024 with a vision to create a flexible professional platform where training, business consultancy, career counselling, coaching, soft skills and marketing support can work together instead of separately.

What makes ITEC different

We connect learning with real life.

Instead of separating training, business, career and communication, ITEC connects them. An individual may need career direction and soft skills. A startup may need strategy and marketing. An organization may need team development, digital support and structured consultancy.
